Privacy policy

clips desk · last updated 9 November 2026

Who uses clips desk

clips desk is a private tool run by the owner of the clips+ fan account, for that account only. It has no other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it can do with Google

When the owner connects the clips+ YouTube channel, Google gives clips desk a key with one permission: to upload videos (youtube.upload). clips desk uses it only to upload clips+ videos: ones the owner tapped Publish on, or of a kind the owner switched on for automatic posting. It doesn't read the channel's other videos, subscribers, viewing figures or any personal information, and it couldn't with that key.

If the owner connects replies, a second, separate key lets clips desk read the comments on clips+ videos and post a reply the owner has read and tapped Send on (youtube.force-ssl). It keeps only a comment's text, its ID and its time, never who wrote it, and deletes them after 7 days. It never edits or deletes videos or comments, although Google's permission would allow it.

What it can do with Meta

For Instagram, a key for the clips+ Facebook Page lets clips desk publish clips+ Reels, the same way as on YouTube, and read back each Reel's link. It doesn't read followers.

If the owner connects replies, separate keys let clips desk read comments on clips+ posts and messages people send clips+ first, and send a reply the owner has read and tapped Send on. For a comment it keeps only the text, its ID and its time, never who wrote it. For a message, the sender's name is shown to the owner beside the draft. Both are deleted after 7 days. It never hides, deletes or likes comments, and never starts a conversation.

What is kept, and where

  • The keys are kept in a locked file on the owner's own server. They're never shared, sold, or used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • A video waiting to be published, and its title and caption, stay on that server. The video is deleted a week after it goes up, or after a month if it never does.
  • A short record of what was posted (the title, the caption, the link and the time) is kept so the same video is never posted twice.
  • Comment and message text kept for drafting replies is deleted after 7 days.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. A video and its words are sent only to YouTube or Instagram. To draft a reply, the text of a comment or message is sent to Anthropic's AI service (Claude), which writes the draft and doesn't keep it to train its models. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Google API Services User Data Policy

clips desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Taking the permission back

The clips+ account can remove clips desk's access at any time: on Google at Google Account third-party connections, and on Meta in Facebook's settings under Business integrations. clips desk then can't post anything.

Changes

Any change to this policy is dated at the top of this page.
