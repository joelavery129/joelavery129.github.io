clips desk

The private posting tool for the clips+ fan account: GTA 6 news, memes and clips.

What it does

clips desk is used by one person: the owner of clips+. Short videos made for clips+, with their titles and captions, arrive on the owner's private dashboard, where the owner watches each one. A video goes to the clips+ YouTube channel as a Short, or to the clips+ Instagram account as a Reel, when the owner taps Publish on it.

The owner can also switch on automatic posting for a few kinds of post (news, campaign clips and the owner's own gameplay). A kind posts by itself only after the owner has approved ten of that kind in a row, at most three a day on each platform, and one tap switches it all off.

clips desk can also read the comments and messages people send clips+ and draft replies. A reply is only sent after the owner has read it and tapped Send.

What it never does

  • It never sends a reply or a message its owner hasn't read and tapped Send on, and never starts a conversation.
  • It never edits or deletes videos, and never hides, deletes or likes anyone's comments.
  • It never posts for anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Signing in with Google

clips desk asks Google for "Manage your YouTube videos" (youtube.upload) on the clips+ channel only, and uses it only to upload clips+ videos. If the owner connects replies, it also asks, separately, for permission to read and reply to comments on clips+ videos (youtube.force-ssl), which it uses for nothing else. How these permissions and everything else are handled is set out in the privacy policy.
